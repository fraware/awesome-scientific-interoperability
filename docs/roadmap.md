# Roadmap

## Initial Release

- Publish the 75-entry seed list and machine-readable catalog.
- Keep the README manually edited and enforce catalog parity in CI.
- Collect corrections and narrowly scoped additions through structured issue and pull-request templates.

## First Maintenance Cycle

- Recheck all canonical URLs and maintenance signals.
- Review the watchlist, especially laboratory and scientific-agent protocols.
- Add missing cross-domain mappings, conformance systems, and non-life-science exemplars when they meet the quality bar.
- Remove redundant implementations as stronger standards or test suites mature.

## Central Awesome Submission

After at least 30 days of public maintenance, run `awesome-lint`, review the current central submission requirements, and submit only when the list has demonstrated active curation and human editorial ownership.
